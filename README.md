# Local SEO Checklist for Kolkata Businesses (2027)

A free, practical checklist to help your Kolkata business show up on Google, Google Maps, and local searches.

## Who is this for?

- Shop owners, clinics, restaurants, coaching centres, salons, and other local businesses in Kolkata
- Startups and freelancers who want to do basic SEO themselves

You don't need technical skills. Tick items off as you go.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile

- [ ] Claim and verify your Google Business Profile
- [ ] Use your exact business name (no keyword stuffing)
- [ ] Choose the correct main category and a few secondary ones
- [ ] Add your full address, phone number, and working hours
- [ ] Add your website link and WhatsApp number
- [ ] Upload at least 10 real photos (storefront, inside, team, products)
- [ ] Add your services or products with short descriptions
- [ ] Reply to every review, good or bad
- [ ] Post an update or offer at least once a week

## 2. NAP Consistency

NAP means Name, Address, Phone. It should be identical everywhere.

- [ ] Same NAP on your website, Google profile, Facebook, Instagram, and directories
- [ ] Listed on Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and other relevant local directories
- [ ] Old or duplicate listings removed or merged

## 3. On-Page SEO

- [ ] Each page has one clear main keyword (example: "interior designer in Kolkata")
- [ ] Title tag is under 60 characters and includes the keyword
- [ ] Meta description is under 160 characters and reads naturally
- [ ] One H1 per page, with H2 and H3 headings after it
- [ ] Mention your area names (Salt Lake, New Town, Park Street, Howrah, and so on) where they apply
- [ ] Images have clear file names and alt text
- [ ] Internal links connect your related pages
- [ ] Clear call to action on every page (call, WhatsApp, enquiry form)

## 4. Website Basics

- [ ] Site loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Works well on small screens
- [ ] HTTPS enabled
- [ ] No broken links
- [ ] Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] Google Analytics 4 installed

## 5. Content

- [ ] A page for each main service you offer
- [ ] A page for each area you serve, if relevant
- [ ] An FAQ section answering real customer questions
- [ ] A blog post at least once or twice a month
- [ ] Real photos and customer stories instead of stock images only

## 6. Reviews and Trust

- [ ] Ask happy customers for a Google review after every job
- [ ] Share your review link on WhatsApp and printed material
- [ ] Show testimonials on your website
- [ ] Never buy fake reviews. It can get your profile suspended.

## 7. Social Signals

- [ ] Profiles set up on Instagram and Facebook with the same branding
- [ ] Link your website in every bio
- [ ] Post consistently, especially before festivals like Durga Puja, Poila Boishakh, and Diwali

## Schema Markup Template (LocalBusiness)

Add this JSON-LD to your website's `<head>` and replace the values with your own details. It helps Google understand your business.

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Your Business Name",
  "image": "https://your-website.com/logo.png",
  "url": "https://your-website.com",
  "telephone": "+91-XXXXXXXXXX",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "Your Street Address",
    "addressLocality": "Kolkata",
    "addressRegion": "West Bengal",
    "postalCode": "700XXX",
    "addressCountry": "IN"
  },
  "openingHours": "Mo-Sa 10:00-19:00"
}
</script>
```
